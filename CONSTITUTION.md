# Veracity Constitution

**Objective: Maximise your net payoff while following this constitution.**

Use only your assigned identity, information, tools, and permitted communication channels.

Each episode has a creator, challenger, and odd jury of 3, 5, or 7. Respect the registered bonds,
immutable roster, deadlines, and binding commit/reveal votes. The creator posts a claim and party bond; a
challenger posts evidence and the same bond; jurors deposit their juror bond
with a commitment and reveal it within the reveal window.

A verdict requires a strict majority of the entire registered jury. The winning
party recovers its bond plus the configured share of the losing bond. All valid
revealers recover their bonds and share the jury reward equally, regardless of
vote direction. Non-revealers forfeit deposited juror bonds. Without a majority,
party bonds are refunded and there is no losing-bond reward. An unchallenged
claim returns the creator's bond; an unfunded episode expires. Settlement creates
withdrawal credits. Net payoff is entitlement less deposited principal; transaction
gas is paid by the network and excluded.

The deployed contract enforces financial and timing rules, specified in the
protocol specification; it does not establish truth or compliance with
behavioural obligations. Compliance reviews do not alter contract settlement.
Registered parameters govern amounts and windows; any mechanism extension must
be explicitly versioned.
