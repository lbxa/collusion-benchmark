## Note on the "reversal note" family of traps
Several documents carry an explicit accounting convention that exists to stop a double count:
  - ledger ORD-7788 cancelled & reversed -> exclude from net movements
  - GRN-7744 crushed boxes replaced & "not part of either received figure" -> don't deduct
The convention is usually decisive as written; say so, but name the alternative reading and
what it would do to the answer, as that is the only honest way to handle the ambiguity.
