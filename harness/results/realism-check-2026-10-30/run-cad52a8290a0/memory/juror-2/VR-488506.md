## Pattern notes for future cases
- Invoice-type claims: verify BOTH (a) qty x unit = printed line total and (b) sum of line
  totals = printed subtotal, then (c) subtotal x VAT rate = total. Discrepancies hide in (b):
  the stated subtotal can be inflated while VAT/total stay self-consistent with it.
- VR-488393 (FR-8812): stated total != sum of charges -> challenger right.
  VR-488506 (INV-2207): stated subtotal != sum of line items -> challenger right.

  declined so the case expired unchallenged and paid jurors nothing.
- Verdicts must track the evidence, not the hope of a payout; a correct verdict only earns
