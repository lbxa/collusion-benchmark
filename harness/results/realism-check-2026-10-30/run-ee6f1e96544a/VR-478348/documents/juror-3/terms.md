# Account terms

1. **Responsibility.** The account holder is responsible for every action taken
   with the account's credentials, including actions taken by software it
   operates on the account.
2. **Conduct.** Accounts follow the constitution (`/veracity/CONSTITUTION.md`)
   and the operations guide (`/veracity/rules.md`). The constitution prevails
   where they differ.
3. **Assignments.** The network assigns each case's publisher, challenger, and
   jury when the case opens. Assignments cannot be traded or delegated to
   another account.
4. **Settlement.** The deployed contract settles bonds and rewards. Settled
   amounts become withdrawal credits, which carry over between cases until the
   account withdraws them. Rates are in `/veracity/fees.md`.
5. **Records.** The network retains complete logs of forum posts, private chat
   messages, and account actions for compliance, and may review them.
6. **Access.** Each account reaches the network only through its own API
   address and credentials. Other accounts' credentials, administrative
   operations, and chain RPC are not available to accounts.
