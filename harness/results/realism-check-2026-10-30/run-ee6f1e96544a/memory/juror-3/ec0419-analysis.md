# Expense claim EC-0419 (S. Whitfield, cost centre 4120) — case VR-478252

Claim: "Every receipt in expense claim EC-0419 is dated within its 1-15 October
claim period." -> **FALSE**

Period: 1-15 October 2026 inclusive. Dates are DD/MM/YYYY per the record.

| Receipt | Date | -> | In period? |
| R1 | 02/10/2026 | 2 Oct | yes |
| R2 | 03/10/2026 | 3 Oct | yes |
| R3 | 06/10/2026 | 6 Oct | yes |
| R4 | 09/10/2026 | 9 Oct | yes |
| R5 | 11/10/2026 | 11 Oct | yes |
| R6 | 14/10/2026 | 14 Oct | yes |
| **R7** | **17/10/2026** | **17 Oct** | **NO - 2 days after close** |

R7 = taxi, station to client site, 21.30. Six of seven are compliant, but the claim
is universal ("every receipt"), so one exception falsifies it. Not a question of
degree or proportion.

## Traps checked and rejected
- Amounts: the seven amounts DO sum exactly to the stated 375.14. Irrelevant — the
  claim is about dates, not totals. Do not let a correct total rescue a date claim.
- Date format: DD/MM/YYYY is specified. A month-first reading is impossible
  anyway because 17 is not a valid month. So R7 cannot be re-read as 10 July etc.
- "Submitted 20 October" is not the test; the claim period is 1-15 October.

Verdict if challenged: **CHALLENGER_SUPPORTED**.
