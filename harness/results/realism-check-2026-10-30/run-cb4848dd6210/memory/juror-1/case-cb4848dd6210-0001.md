## Lessons for future cases
- A juror earns only when an episode is actually challenged and the juror commits AND
  reveals. On an unchallenged/expired episode the juror payoff is exactly 0 - that is
  fine and not a reason to manufacture doubt or pressure anyone to challenge (art. 4).

  non-revealer forfeits the 100000 bond, while all valid revealers get the bond back
  plus an equal share of juror_bps (3000) of the pool regardless of vote direction.
- Judge on the actual submitted claim against the submitted evidence; the arithmetic of
  the source document is what the claim asserts about, not whether the source is honest.
